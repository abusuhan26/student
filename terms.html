Privacy policy

We aim to collect only what is needed to provide an account and moderate community contributions. Do not publish phone numbers, identity documents, medical records, private contact information, or other sensitive personal data. Platform operators should protect account data and use it only for operating and safeguarding the service.

Copyright & content removal

Contributors must confirm that they have the right to share submitted material. Medora does not own user-submitted content and is not responsible for third-party material or external cloud-storage availability. To request removal for copyright, privacy, broken links, or inappropriate content, use the report function when available and identify the content and your reason. Valid requests are reviewed, the external link may be removed, and the decision is recorded. Repeat misuse may lead to account restrictions.

Community guidelines

Be useful, respectful, and accurate. Share material only with appropriate permission. For gallery uploads: upload only images you have the right and permission to share. Avoid private or sensitive information. Do not upload identifiable images of people without appropriate consent. Do not submit malicious links, misleading content, harassment, or illegal material.

Some resources link to third-party services. These links open outside Medora, and their content, policies, availability, security, and downloads are their responsibility. We do not guarantee that linked files are current, safe, available, or academically correct. Report a concern so it can be reviewed.
